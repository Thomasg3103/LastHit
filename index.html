  • It's blind. It hunts by smell and sound. Every hit leaves a cloud it can follow. Running, slamming doors and your ringing phone bring it running.
  • Hold your breath when it's in the room. After a couple of silent seconds it loses you and leaves. Run out of air and you'll cough.
  • Once you're Geeked (60% high), a door you came in through won't lead back where it did. The house rearranges itself.
  • The living room is safe. It never comes in, and the house resets when you go back.
  • The sensor beeps faster the closer it gets. Once you're high, don't trust it completely.
  • Pick up snacks and eat them when you need to. Eating brings your high down, but chips and soda are loud.
  • Empty bottles and cans make good decoys. Climb into a wardrobe, under a bed or into a bathtub and it can't find you, unless it was close enough to hear you get in.
  • Every item you find is a save point, but each one leaves you a little higher for good. Hit 100% and you green out.
